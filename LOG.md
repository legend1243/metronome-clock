# Development Journal

## 15 Nov 2026 (~1 hour)

- Selected the **ATtiny412** as the project's microcontroller.
- Completed the basic power supply schematic, supporting both coin-cell battery power and an alternative external power input through the pin header.
- Added a 0.1µF decoupling capacitor between VCC and GND.
- Added a power indicator LED.
- Connected the microcontroller GPIO pins to an 8-pin connector (01x08).
- Added a safety resistor to the UPDI programming interface.

## 16 Nov 2026 (~3 hours)

- Completed the initial schematic design.
- Connected GPIO pins to the buzzer and indicator LEDs.
- Cleaned up and organized the schematic.
- Assigned footprints to all components.
- Completed initial component placement on the PCB.
- Routed PCB traces using a two-layer layout.
- Ran and passed the Design Rule Check (DRC).
- Added decorative elements to the PCB layout.

## 17 Nov 2026 (~5 hours)

- Redesigned the schematic and PCB layout.
- Replaced the initial LED-based display with a 1.3-inch OLED screen.
- Changed the power supply from a coin-cell battery to two AAA batteries.
- Generated the initial Bill of Materials (BOM).

## 20 Nov 2026 (~1 hour)

- Updated and refined the Bill of Materials (BOM).
- Updated the project README and development journal.
